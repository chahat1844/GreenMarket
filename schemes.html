✅ Common Eligibility Requirements

  • ✔️ Must be a registered farmer (land record required)
  • ✔️ Valid Aadhar Card linked to bank account
  • ✔️ Kisan Credit Card (for loan-related schemes)
  • ✔️ Land ownership or tenancy proof
  • ✔️ Bank account with IFSC code
  • ✔️ Mobile number linked to Aadhar
  • ✔️ Soil Health Card (for soil-related subsidies)
  • ✔️ PM-Kisan registration (for income support)
